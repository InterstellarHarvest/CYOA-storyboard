# Chapter 5 — owner ruling 04: no cooperative shutdown
## Corrected controlling rule
Nate clarified: "you may be able to sabotage something, but nobody is going to follow a request to shut it down. they dont see a reason to, dont know how to, arent allowed to, etc."
The earlier interpretation that the demonstration is physically impossible to shut off was too broad and is superseded.
Nobody cooperatively disables the demonstration at the player's request. Depending on NPC, refusal may reflect complacency, ignorance, lack of authority, or unwillingness.
Sabotage, damage, tampering or unauthorized interruption remain possible design directions. Their exact effects, detection, checks, casualties and persistent consequences require authoring and owner review.
Do not invent invulnerability, backup power, an indestructible installation or universal sabotage failure to enforce the social refusal.
Normal shutdown on request remains rejected. An ask-to-switch-off dialogue may receive a short refusal but is not a successful solution or repeatable question loop.
Cabinet remains a viable tampering branch. Labels/controls may be retained or revised to support concrete approved consequences; no shutdown success or failure is determined yet.
