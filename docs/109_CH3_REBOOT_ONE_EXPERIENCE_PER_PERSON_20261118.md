## Why the previous chapter was retired

The owner found the existing Chapter 3 repetitive, cold, over-bureaucratic, and insufficiently ridiculous or meaningful. Its many records offices, corrective procedures, and long identity-certification finale did not create a compelling sequence of incidents. More importantly, the earlier creative team misinterpreted the "Original Witness" as a potentially embodied autonomous person. That story direction is **superseded**.

The previous 62-node chapter, associated choices/endings/death-popup drafts and old art briefs are now **historical material only**. Their old `owner-approved` notes record real earlier selections, but those selections were overtaken by the owner's explicit Chapter 3 reboot decision. Preserve them, do not silently re-author them as current canon.

## Approved creative foundation (locked)

1. **Simple objective:** The protagonist needs to use the bathroom. This remains the chapter's practical motivation and the game's running gag.
2. **A genuinely functional bathroom:** At the People's Pavilion the protagonist encounters a real, working restroom, visibly used by other people, rather than another decorative false toilet.
3. **One experience per person:** Pavilion policy allows each person a single experience of using this restroom. The protagonist's one permitted experience is recorded as already redeemed.
4. **False identity record:** The Pavilion claims an official "Original" matching the protagonist has already used the restroom. The player insists they are themselves; official evidence says they are not the certified person. The "other you" is **a datum or record**, not an established physical clone, witness, robot, or companion. Do not introduce an embodied Original or imply one definitely exists.
5. **Episodic absurdity, not a grand investigation:** A small number of funny, physical, increasingly ridiculous tests, demonstrations, incidents and human encounters follow from trying to get in. Strange societal assumptions may be hinted at without a conspiracy exposition or major lore reveal.
6. **Mandatory unresolved outcome:** Every surviving route leaves the Pavilion without using the bathroom and **without discovering who or what caused the Original entry**. The identity oddity may matter in a later chapter, but Chapter 3 does not solve it. Death routes may remain possible where fairly signaled.
7. **Forward-moving CYOA:** Distinct choices should cause different incidents, tangible consequences, meaningful item/condition callbacks or exits—not repeated application loops. Use hidden d6 checks when actual risk is meaningful, not for routine paperwork.
8. **Tone and world:** Comedy and institutional stupidity first, unsettling implications underneath. Actual human NPCs with personalities, strong dialogue and portraits should appear; the place can still be strangely quiet. Never reveal the protagonist's face. Narration uses second-person present tense.

**Important distinction:** The owner has approved the *direction*, not the details of any specific new test, NPC, exit, check odds, item consumption, or scene prose. Those remain collaborative decisions.

## Proposed compact narrative spine (NOT LOCKED)

### Incident 1 — The Restroom Refusal

The protagonist arrives through one of the existing Chapter 2 exit groups (`public_concourse`, `service_infrastructure`, or `underground_transit`). They find a working restroom: an actual visitor leaves, the tap runs, and a toilet audibly flushes. A reader or human attendant rejects the protagonist's entry: `ONE EXPERIENCE PER PERSON — ALREADY REDEEMED`. The official record says the certified Original has already enjoyed this visit. The person in front of the door cannot be that Original.

The one-use rule need not receive a coherent explanation. Its institutional enforcement is the immediate joke; the false identity match is a lingering oddity, not the objective of a grand detective plot.

### Incident 2 — Human Verification

An employee or exhibition system offers some way to dispute the claim. Instead of repeated offices, the protagonist encounters actual Pavilion demonstrations of "authentic" humanity. Consider salvaging **Spontaneous Laughter**, **Genuine Surprise**, and the **Free Will** buttons (`I AGREE` / `I AGREE ENTHUSIASTICALLY`). Their absurd logic should drive physical comedy, dialogue and consequences; do not simply recreate the legacy three-hub administrative graph.

### Incident 3 — Things Get Out of Hand

Branches can lead to a performance malfunction, an overzealous human employee, an unintended public demonstration, a mechanical mishap, or a classification surprise. At least one earlier chapter item or condition should be allowed to alter a meaningful outcome where plausible; it must not be required for baseline survival.

### Incident 4 — The Unceremonious Departure

Different routes end with being escorted out, ejected by the Pavilion, transferred out under a ridiculous classification, escaping through service infrastructure, or another logically earned departure. An optional dangerous action may cause a clearly warned fatal result. Do not force every path through a Grand Reconciliation hearing. Every survivor still needs a bathroom; no route reveals the person or origin behind the certified record.

These four headings are an **organizational proposal**, not fixed incident count. Aim for a shorter, funnier chapter, comparable in actual play time and consequence depth to Chapters 1–2, without padding for a node or choice quota.

## Candidate material worth salvaging — not automatically canon

- The **Human Behavior Workshop**'s scheduled laughter, countdown surprise, and compulsory agreement buttons.
- One good human character such as **Mrs. Pruitt**, who can recognize a contradiction without becoming the narrator of a giant mystery.
- The **Actual Events / Approved Events** visual gag, if it fits a physical incident rather than another department tour.
- A revised **A MODEL CITIZEN** fatal concept, only if the new story earns the warning, player choice and consequence. The legacy mechanism and popup are *not* carried forward automatically.
- Earlier-chapter effects: family photograph; registered-as-equipment status; Examiner/Acting Director credentials; held stapler/clicker/rubber-band ball; viable seed canister; botanical infection or a full cure; tram-related paperwork. Use only meaningful, reachable conditional payoffs and respect lost items. These are opportunities, not a checklist.

**Not carried forward:** embodied Original Witness, autonomous private naming and independent escape, compulsory Grand Reconciliation, the many near-identical correction/records offices, duplicate fake bathroom, and any automatic Chapter 4 revelation or mandatory mystery explanation.

## Proposed opening vignette (UNAPPROVED SAMPLE PROSE)

You emerge into the People's Pavilion beneath a sign reading PUBLIC RESTROOMS.

A man comes out of the door, drying his hands with a paper towel. Behind him, a toilet flushes. Real water runs through real pipes. For a brief, unreasonable moment, you are happy.

You reach for the handle. A green light turns red.

ONE EXPERIENCE PER PERSON — ALREADY REDEEMED.

The attendant glances at a punched card. Your restroom visit, it seems, was completed by the certified Original. You explain that you have not been inside. He agrees that you have not. That's precisely why he can't let you in: the person authorized to use it has already done so, and you aren't that person.

The man with the paper towel looks from you to the card.

"Oh," he says. "You're one of those."

You ask what that means.

He folds the paper towel very carefully. "Wouldn't know. Never happened to me."

The door locks behind him.

### CH3-003A — Authentic Urgency Exhibit

The player shouts that they have needed the bathroom all day. Their voice booms through the auditorium. The presenter mistakes the complaint for a splendid live illustration of an authentic human need, sounds a bell and lowers red curtains around the stage.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION — THE EXPERIENCE OF URGENCY.**

She asks the lone spectator to observe the unmistakable signs of a desperate human. When the protagonist objects to being watched, the presenter celebrates **PUBLIC EMBARRASSMENT** as a second authentic human experience. She invites them to consider performing professionally. The platform begins rotating away from the restroom; the protagonist can still see the real bathroom through the curtains.

**Continuity:** Embarrassing live-exhibit situation; stage movement creates a new physical possibility. It does not certify the player's identity or grant bathroom access. The immediate next choices, whether a visitor interferes, and the final exit are unapproved.

### CH3-003B — Human Locomotion Demonstration

The player tries to step off the platform. Its floor moves beneath them, matching their pace as a cheerful sign announces **HUMAN LOCOMOTION DEMONSTRATION INITIATED**. The presenter celebrates walking, then running, as the protagonist speeds up. A safety net begins unfolding above a padded demonstration pit.

The presenter suggests the next portion may involve falling, while the platform approaches its edge.

**Continuity:** A meaningful physical hidden-d6 check is proposed here. Success may give access to a service passage; failure may deposit the protagonist in a ridiculous safety demonstration or an escorted exit. No check threshold, item modifier, injury, death or actual destination is yet approved. There is **no** loop back to the original identity-test menu.

### CH3-003C — Unauthorized Presenter

The player challenges the presenter's own identity. She appeals to the camera. It reports **PRESENTER IDENTITY: UNCONFIRMED**, then **AUTHORIZED PRESENTER: CURRENTLY ON SCHEDULED BREAK**. She protests that her break hasn't started. An alarm declares **UNAUTHORIZED PRESENTER DETECTED**. Her platform begins rising while the microphone retracts out of her hand.

A second microphone drops before the protagonist. A sign announces **TEMPORARY PRESENTER APPOINTED**. The genuine presenter, now the unwilling subject of the verification demonstration, begs the protagonist not to press anything. A large green button illuminates: **BEGIN DEMONSTRATION**.

**Continuity:** The protagonist is accidentally placed in the presenter's role; the woman is still a genuine, vulnerable human, not a machine or an Original identity manifestation. Her personality and the player's consequential choices are to be developed. No administrative authority outside the room is granted merely by holding this microphone.

**Owner approval scope:** The three incidents above and their general cause-and-effect are retained. All three are consequences of CH3-002A choices, with no forced A→B→C order. Further decision cards, final route convergence and precise gameplay data are not yet approved.
