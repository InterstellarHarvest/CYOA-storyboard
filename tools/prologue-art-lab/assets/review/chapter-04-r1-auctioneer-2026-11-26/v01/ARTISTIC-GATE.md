## What the study demonstrates

The image was painted from a blank 564×278 RGBA canvas using 272 explicit native
brush marks in nine editable raster layers. The paint tape reproduces every layer
and the composite exactly. It does not read reference image pixels. There is no
resampling, automatic quantization, opacity repair, random texture or mathematical
lighting field in the painting process. All figure alpha values are 255 and all
empty pixels are 0. The palette contains 60 deliberately assigned material entries;
54 colours remain visible after layer coverage. It has not been padded to reach a
numerical target.

That proves a different technical construction, **not sufficient illustration
quality**. This scripted placement of native brush marks still inherits the
essential artistic limitation of the earlier coordinate-drawing approach.
