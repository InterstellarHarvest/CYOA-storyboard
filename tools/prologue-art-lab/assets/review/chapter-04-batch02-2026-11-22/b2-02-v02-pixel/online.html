What to inspect

Judge native size first, then exact4x: intentional clusters and stable edges; readable architecture/objects; distinct plaster, wood, velvet, steel, piano and stone; stepped light/shadow; simple scene-NPC silhouettes. Color counts and passing file checks do not establish visual approval.

Dock and stairs established the treatment first. The four layouts retain the shipping staircase, sofa/trolley, chandelier hoist/padded crate, two piano workers plus foreman, cake trolley and door/stair geometry. Floor perspective and light/shadow regions are constructed on the final grid, not copied photographic grain. Material masks and individual NPC-region layers are saved for targeted revision.

The foreman and portrait background remain their exact v01 files, shown below as retained UNSELECTED candidates; this pass does not flatten them to main-scene detail.
