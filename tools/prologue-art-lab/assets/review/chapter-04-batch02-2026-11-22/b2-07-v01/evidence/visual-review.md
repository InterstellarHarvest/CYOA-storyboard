Reviewed the actual PNGs against the exact canonical Chapter2 Bureau/Control Tower, approved Chapter4 restroom/service passage and approved Chapter3 Pavilion attendant/presenter and Chapter4 bride. Palette totals and technical dimensions were not used as artistic approval.

- **Main scene:** doorway, verticals and floor depth read coherently; the assistant has adult scale and a full planted silhouette, not tiny plumber proportions. Main NPC eyes/hands are concise clusters. Ceramic WC, teal tiles/duct, copper joints, gold fixtures, cream plaster and cardboard retain distinct planes. Source-to-native comparison retains cistern, flush lever, place cards, complete small NPC/shoes, side boxes and WC. Central entry occlusion is an explicit box layer, never an accidental structural wipe.

Warm lamp illumination remains localized, not concentric procedural halos. Important fixtures are excluded from plaster remapping. Quieter central/right wall supports portraits.
- **Story states:** red ribbon is explicitly restored/protected after ceramic material fitting. The closed lid/tag survives under the entry occluder. Revealed tag uses full canonical copy in chunky bitmap capitals with clean margins, projected into a larger hanging paper plane so small lines stay legible. Native paper text and WC remain visible beside the character. Open-lid frame removes ribbon/tag, preserves a visible upright lid and places one clipboard on the seat. Bounded state masks preserve all surrounding pixels. These are conditional references, not new runtime requirements.
- **Assistant:** one face/hair/uniform identity across all poses. Busy has both hands sorting cards. Sympathetic offers an empty reassuring hand. Apologetic has empty clasped hands; the clipboard belongs to the seat in that final story beat. Eyes, brows, nose, mouth, curled hair mass, hands/fingers, collar, seams/buttons and belt survive native transfer. Exact4× shows stepped ramps and clean binary silhouette, without a colored fringe or smooth resampling. Approved Pavilion portraits provide the perceived human rendering benchmark; Chapter2 supplies cluster/material craft.

All intermediate rendering runs remain unpublished experiments; only the strongest completed native result is offered in v01.
