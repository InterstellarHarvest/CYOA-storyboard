## Unmet technical requirements

**Original native-grid artwork: FAIL for all six.** The tool returned 1548×1016 main scenes and approximately 1785–1786×881 portrait sources despite the exact catalogue prompts. The originals are preserved; candidates are full-frame NEAREST review derivatives at the requested dimensions. There is no evidence that the source was authored on the requested logical pixel grid. Fine tonal variation and nonuniform source pixel density remain; review exports contain approximately 10,000–33,000 visible RGB colors. No palette reduction was applied to conceal this.

**Strict pixel-sharp alpha: FAIL for both foregrounds.** Actual alpha is preserved. The attendant export has 43,336 partial-alpha pixels and the presenter 42,201. Most visible sprite pixels are not strictly 255 alpha. Transparent exterior regions and valid composites pass, but these are not binary-alpha production sprites. White/dark proofs are supplied to expose edge behavior. No thresholding or hand-painted cleanup has been performed.

Correct dimensions, opaque environment exports, exact 4× nearest-neighbor previews, source preservation, provenance and literal layer-composite checks pass. Those checks do not confer creative approval or remedy source-grid/alpha failures. Nate's explicit selection will constitute creative approval of the exact selected candidate, per the handoff; any unresolved technical defects remain documented for subsequent production work.
